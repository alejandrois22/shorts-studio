Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio Autopilot · effective 2026-11-08 · about the tool

Shorts Studio Autopilot is a private, single-user software tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to, and read statistics and comments from, the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and is not distributed to anyone else.

1. What the tool accesses

Through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, authenticated with the owner's own Google account via OAuth 2.0, the tool:

  • uploads the owner's own videos with the title, description and tags the owner prepared;
  • reads public statistics (views, likes, subscriber counts) and analytics reports for the owner's own channels and videos;
  • reads comments posted on the owner's own videos and posts the owner's replies to them;
  • updates metadata (title, description, category, privacy status) of the owner's own videos.

Scopes used: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl, yt-analytics.readonly.

2. Data about other people

The only data about people other than the owner that the tool processes is the public content of comments on the owner's videos (comment text, display name, channel id), fetched so the owner can answer them. This data is cached on the owner's computer for up to 30 days, is used solely to display and answer comments, and is never sold, shared, combined with other sources, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

3. Storage and security

All data (OAuth tokens, cached statistics, comment cache, a local SQLite database) is stored only on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in a local folder outside any shared or published directory. No data is sent to third-party servers other than Google's APIs, except an optional push notification to the owner's own phone containing aggregate view counts.

4. Cookies and tracking

The tool has no public website, uses no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Google's policies

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The tool complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

6. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; revocation immediately stops all API access, and local caches can be deleted by removing the tool's data folder.

If you commented on one of the owner's videos and want your comment removed from the local cache, email the address below; it is deleted within 7 days (deleting your comment on YouTube also removes it from the cache at the next refresh).
