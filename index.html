Shorts Studio Autopilot

A private publishing tool for one creator's own YouTube channels · Privacy policy

Shorts Studio Autopilot is a single-user program that runs on its owner's computer. It renders short educational animations (how everyday objects work, body-science "try it" experiments, 3D cutaways, a GTA 6 countdown fan series), checks them against quality rules, and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels on a steady schedule. It then reads the channels' own statistics into a local dashboard and helps the owner answer viewers' comments.

Channels it publishes to

  • Doodle Brain — hidden engineering and try-it Shorts
  • Curious Doodle — try-it Shorts
  • Mini Mentes — 3D how-to and what-if Shorts
  • Leonida Countdown — GTA 6 countdown fan channel (not affiliated with Rockstar Games)

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • Uploads the owner's own videos at scheduled times (YouTube Data API, videos.insert).
  • Reads the owner's own view, like and subscriber counts every few minutes for a local dashboard (videos.list, channels.list).
  • Reads comments on the owner's videos and posts the owner's replies (commentThreads.list, comments.insert).
  • Reads retention and audience reports for the owner's channels (YouTube Analytics API).

It is not available to other users, stores all data locally, and does not collect, sell or share data about anyone other than the public comments viewers leave on the owner's videos. Details: privacy policy.
